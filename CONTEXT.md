# Sundarbans House

This glossary records the people, permissions, and content terms used while redesigning Sundarbans House as a student utility.

## People and governance

**WebAdmin**:
The UHC's technical office-holder responsible for the site, configuration and recovery, with super-admin access.

**Super admin**:
One of exactly three fixed house-admin email accounts with member, role, account-control and approval powers for Secretary, Deputy Secretary and WebAdmin. The people operating these accounts may change while the login emails remain the same.
_Avoid_: treating super admin as a public council title or calling every super admin WebAdmin.

**Two-person approval**:
Approval of an authorized admin's change to another student's details, account status or regional/community admin role by a different super admin. A super admin's own request also needs another super admin's approval.

**Upper House Council (UHC)**:
The council consisting of Secretary, Deputy Secretary and WebAdmin, whose office-holders use the three fixed super-admin accounts.

**Admin**:
An umbrella term for an operator with explicitly assigned management permissions, including super admins, regional operators and community operators; its scope must be stated.

**Regional Coordinator (RC)**:
A regional operator who manages meetups and region-specific updates for an assigned region and may approve region-change requests from its current members. A region may have zero, one or two active RCs.

**International region**:
The regional allocation group for members listed as International Region. It has no Regional Coordinator.

**Community Head / Co-Head**:
An assigned community operator who can create, edit and directly publish that community's events. Both roles have the same event controls; super admins retain oversight across communities. A community has one Head and at most two Co-Heads.

**Group application**:
A student's submitted house-themed form requesting access to a community or group. Submission reveals the WhatsApp invite, but does not establish verified group membership.

**Verified group admission**:
Entry approved in WhatsApp by a super admin or admin after checking house membership against the Google Sheet and, for a regional group, regional eligibility.

**Event registration**:
A student's submission of the event's house-themed registration form. Registration is required for a participation certificate, but does not prove attendance.

**Event attendance**:
A student's recorded participation in an event, evidenced by its uploaded attendance report and matched to registration by email.

**Participation certificate release**:
Authorization by the event's responsible organizer for eligible registered attendees to generate/download certificates using its signed template. Super admins retain oversight.

**Public visitor**:
Any student or visitor using the public site without signing in.

**Member**:
A person on the active member roster. Membership grants access to the Members Lounge; it is not a council operator role.

**Member roster**:
The council-maintained list of members, kept in the council-designated Google Sheet. Repeated entries for the same email represent one member; approved additions are made individually or imported in bulk, rather than automatically synchronized.

**Pending student**:
A student whose email is on the approved member roster but who has not yet signed in to the Lounge.

**Members Lounge**:
The members-only area of the site, entered by signing in with a Google account whose email is on the active member roster. Its home is about the member: their name, the live event, and their WhatsApp groups. Roll, region and Regional Coordinator live in the member's profile; IITM BS societies are not shown.
_Avoid_: "launch page" (a dictation slip for Lounge); "bots" (a dictation slip for boats, the WhatsApp groups moored at the ghat).

**Welcome tour**:
A journey a member takes before entering the Members Lounge: the house's story, past councils, this year's Upper House Council and the communities. Entering the Lounge or choosing Not now at the ghat marks it seen; Skip tour only advances to the ghat. The intended backend rule is once per member, with replay from the profile. Until backend sign-in is wired, every explicit Sign in replays the tour.

**Past council**:
The Secretary and Deputy Secretary of an earlier year, in that order. Web Admins are not listed per year; the house credits them through milestones.

**Milestone**:
A lasting achievement of the house, shown on its own in the welcome tour with the people who made it, e.g. 2023: moving from a Google Sites page to the house's own website, the first house to do so.

**Preferred name**:
The optional name a member chooses to be called in the Lounge, requested at first entry and addable or editable later. A member who has not supplied one is shown no name; the roll number is never used in its place.

**Certificate name**:
The name a member confirms when first requesting a certificate. It stays separate from their editable Lounge name; later corrections go through a council request.

## Content lifecycle

**Term**:
One of the January, May or September study periods in a year. Actual teaching and examination dates follow its academic calendar and may differ from the label month.
_Avoid_: "academic year" for one four-month term.

**Cohort**:
A group of students identified by entry year and term: F1 is January, F2 May and F3 September. Codes combine the year and term, such as 26F1 for January 2026 or 25F3 for September 2025.

**Cohort audience**:
The entry-term groups a publisher permits to see an upcoming/live event within its region or house-wide scope; All students includes every cohort. Published past event listings are public regardless of their earlier cohort audience.

**Future cohort**:
The immediately next entry-term cohort offered for advance event planning, marked Future. The current cohort advances on January 1, May 1 and September 1, independently of teaching dates. Later future cohorts are not yet offered.

**Public content**:
Content that a public visitor can read without authentication, such as study resources, completed event listings, important dates and the team directory.

**Draft**:
Content being prepared by an operator. It is not visible on public pages.

**Pending review**:
Content held for approval when a content type requires review before publication.

**Published**:
Content deliberately released to its permitted audience. A published event may still be restricted by region or lifecycle stage.

**Event stage**:
An event is Upcoming before its scheduled start, Live from start until end, and Past at its scheduled end and afterward. A published Past event listing is public.

**Region-scoped content**:
Content owned by one region, which an RC may manage within that region.

**Community event**:
An event owned by a community and managed by its Head, Co-Head or a super admin. Upcoming/live listings are visible to signed-in house members across regions, subject to cohort targeting; joining the community is not required. Published past listings are public.
